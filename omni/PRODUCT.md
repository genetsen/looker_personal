# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Media, analytics, and client-service users reviewing Omni dashboards for campaign performance. They need to scan spend, delivery, engagement, and efficiency quickly, then move into supporting charts and tables when a metric needs explanation.

## Product Purpose

This Omni surface turns BigQuery-backed campaign data into clear dashboard widgets for fast performance review. Success means a user can understand the current Ritual topline at a glance without losing confidence in the underlying metrics.

## Brand Personality

Premium, clear, disciplined. The interface should feel aligned with Ritual's confident yellow-and-navy identity while remaining practical enough for repeated analytics work.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard decoration, oversized hero copy, vague marketing slogans, glassy cards, heavy gradients, and large visual flourishes that compete with the metrics. Do not let brand expression reduce scan speed or make values harder to compare.

## Design Principles

- Metrics first: every visual choice should make the KPI values easier to scan, compare, or trust.
- Brand with restraint: use Ritual identity as a disciplined system, not as decoration.
- Compact confidence: the Topline area should feel polished and premium while fitting naturally into the dashboard grid.
- Preserve analysis flow: widget styling should not disrupt the surrounding Omni charts, filters, or tables.
- Change surgically: dashboard edits should stay scoped to the requested widget unless the user asks for broader redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize strong contrast, readable data labels, visible focus states where controls are interactive, and layouts that remain understandable when color alone is unavailable. Avoid motion or decorative effects that distract from task completion.
